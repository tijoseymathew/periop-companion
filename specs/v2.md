# PeriOp Companion — Provider Workflow Platform Specification

**From retrospective review to live clinical workflow: providers create cases, feed each stage its inputs (records at intake, audio at the bedside), and hand the patient — with full provenance — to the next provider. The claim ledger and click-to-play provenance of [specs/ui.md](ui.md) stay the trust surface; this spec adds the write path around them.**

Version: 0.1 (draft for review)
Status: Companion to [specs/v1.md](v1.md) (pipeline) and [specs/ui.md](ui.md) (review UI). Supersedes neither: the pipeline agents and the review workspace are reused as-is; this spec wraps them in a case lifecycle driven from the UI.
Framing unchanged: reference/demonstration project, synthetic data only, no PHI, documentation-support tool — **not a medical device**.

---

## 1. Why this layer

Today the platform is batch-shaped: `synthgen` fabricates a complete case bundle (records + interview audio + voice notes), `run_case` processes all three stages in one pass, and the review UI inspects the finished ledger. That proves the provenance thesis but does not resemble how the tool would actually be used. In reality:

1. **Inputs arrive over time, not as a bundle.** The pre-op evaluation happens days before surgery; intra-op voice notes happen during it; the post-op interview happens in recovery. The workflow must accept inputs stage by stage and generate artifacts as soon as each stage's inputs exist.
2. **The workflow starts with a person, not a JSON file.** An anaesthetist opens a case by providing the patient's history — pasting or uploading prior records and the op plan — and the system responds with the GapAnalyst's clarification questions *before* the patient interview, when they are actually useful.
3. **Audio is captured, not pre-rendered.** The pre-op interview, intra-op voice notes, and post-op interview are recorded (or uploaded) by the provider at the point of care.

### 1.1 Clinical grounding

Four realities of hospital anaesthesia practice reshape the design, and each maps to a concrete requirement:

| Clinical reality | Design consequence |
|---|---|
| The doctor who does the pre-op eval is often not the intra-op anaesthetist, and neither may be the doctor caring for the patient in recovery. | **Provider discontinuity is the normal case, not the exception.** Every stage records who performed it. The previous stage's artifact is the incoming provider's orientation document, and the PACU handoff — already the pipeline's most constrained artifact — becomes the product's centerpiece: a provenance-carrying transfer between two people who may never speak. Handoff gets an explicit acknowledge step (§4.4). |
| The value is highest in high-volume centres, where a large anaesthesia department runs many lists in parallel and cross-covers freely. | The case list becomes a **worklist**: many concurrent cases at different stages, scannable at a glance (stage, status, waiting-on-what), filterable by stage and provider. Single-case demo flows must not be baked into the information architecture. |
| Clinicians resent being made to learn yet another piece of tech — every new system competes with patient care for attention. | **Zero-training UX** is a hard requirement, not polish (§6). At any moment a case shows exactly one primary action ("Record interview", "Generate note", "Hand off"). The workflow is enforced by the UI — a provider can never be in a state where they must know what to do next; the screen tells them. Speech is the primary input because talking to patients and dictating are skills every anaesthetist already has. |
| Adoption has to work for the least tech-inclined member of the department, including senior consultants decades past their last new-system rollout. | The UI reads like the paper it replaces: clinical vocabulary (ASA-PS, SBAR, PACU), forms that look like forms, one big obvious record button, large tap targets, no icon-only actions, no gesture-driven or hidden interactions. If a feature needs explaining, it is redesigned or cut. |

## 2. Scope

### In scope (MVP)
- **Case creation from the UI**: provider starts a case by entering a case label and providing prior records (paste text and/or upload files) plus the operative plan.
- **Stage-gated lifecycle** (§4): each stage unlocks when its inputs exist, runs on demand, and completes with an explicit provider sign-off; the case carries per-stage status, provider attribution, and timestamps.
- **Audio capture and upload**: in-browser recording (MediaRecorder) and file upload for the pre-op interview, intra-op voice notes (multiple, append-style), and post-op interview; server-side normalization to wav for the Parakeet ASR path.
- **Human-in-the-loop question review**: the GapAnalyst's questions are shown to the pre-op provider for edit/approval before the interview — realizing v1 §3.3 step 3, which the batch pipeline skips.
- **Handoff acknowledge**: the receiving provider opens the handoff, can play any cited clip, and acknowledges receipt (recorded on the case).
- **Worklist**: the left sidebar becomes a stage-aware worklist with filters; existing synthetic cases load as seeded demo data and remain fully reviewable.
- **Provider identity, lightweight**: a name/role picker (no auth) whose selection stamps `performed_by` on stages and sign-offs. Enough to demonstrate discontinuity; not an identity system.
- **Live stage runs with progress**: the SSE trace stream specified in ui.md §7 is promoted from stretch to MVP — a provider who taps "Generate note" must see progress, not a spinner of unknown duration (Super 49B stages run minutes, per the committed profiler reports).

### Stretch
- Streaming intra-op ASR (Parakeet streaming profile) instead of per-memo transcription.
- Tablet-width responsive layout for the intra-op recording screen (theatre use is realistically not at a desktop).
- Per-claim review actions (mark reviewed / flag) persisted as sidecar state, feeding the sign-off summary.
- Worklist assignment ("my cases") and a department dashboard view.

### Out of scope
- Real patient data, EHR/FHIR integration, authentication/SSO, role-based access control, audit-grade logging, multi-server deployment, offline capture. Concurrency beyond one pipeline run at a time per server (in-memory lock, as ui.md §7 already states).
- Editing generated prose. Unchanged invariant from ui.md: notes are assembled from claims; review actions operate on claims and stages, never on text.

## 3. Architecture deltas

The layering of v1 §3.1 and ui.md §3 is unchanged. What changes is the direction of data flow: the API grows a write path, and the pipeline's stage functions are invoked one stage at a time instead of end-to-end.

```
┌──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│  ui/ SPA — worklist · case intake · stage panels · capture   │
│  + existing review workspace (claim ledger, provenance)      │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  periop.api — existing read endpoints (ui.md §4)             │
│  + write routers: cases (create) · sources (docs, audio)     │
│    · stages (run w/ SSE, sign-off) · handoff (acknowledge)   │
├──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│  Existing seams, reused unmodified where possible:           │
│  tools/ingest.py (RecordIngestor) · tools/asr.py (Parakeet)  │
│  pipeline.run_preop / run_intraop / run_postop               │
│  agents/* · CaseStore (grows write + workflow state)         │
└──────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

Key structural points:

- **The stage functions are already the right granularity.** `periop.pipeline.run_preop/run_intraop/run_postop` each take a `Case` and return an enriched `Case`, with `_require` guarding stage order. The workflow layer calls them individually when a provider triggers a stage; `run_case_stages` remains for CLI/eval batch use.
- **Live cases and synthetic cases share one schema.** A live case is a `Case` whose sources arrived via the API instead of `synthgen`. Gold artifacts and eval remain synthetic-only; nothing in the eval harness changes.
- **Audio path**: browser capture produces webm/opus; the API transcodes to 16 kHz mono wav (ffmpeg, server-side) into the existing `data/cases/<id>/audio/` convention, so ASR, the audio-serving endpoint, and clip playback all work unchanged. Uploads of wav/m4a/mp3 are accepted and normalized the same way.
- **Diarization role mapping** for live pre-op/post-op interviews reuses the v1 §10 heuristic (the provider asks the approved questions → anchors the PROVIDER speaker). Intra-op memos are single-speaker by construction.

## 4. The case lifecycle

A case moves through stages; each stage has the same internal shape — **inputs → generate → review → sign off** — so a provider who has used one stage has learned all three.

```
created ──► PRE-OP ──────────► INTRA-OP ─────────► POST-OP ──────► closed
            inputs: records,    inputs: voice       inputs: post-op
            interview audio     notes (n≥1)         interview audio
            gate: questions     gate: pre-op        gate: intra-op
            approved            signed off          signed off
            output: pre-anaes   output: intra-op    output: PACU handoff
            note                record + antic.     (+ acknowledge) +
                                issues              post-anaes eval
```

Per-stage status vocabulary (rendered as plain words in the UI, stored on the case): `awaiting inputs` → `ready to generate` → `generating` → `awaiting review` → `signed off`. A case's headline status is its first non-signed-off stage.

### 4.1 Case creation & pre-op intake
1. Provider (identified via the picker) taps **New case**, enters a case label. *(Synthetic-data note rendered on the form: demo identifiers only, never real patient details.)*
2. Provides prior records: paste text into typed slots (GP summary, medication list, previous anaesthetic record, other) and/or upload `.txt`/`.md`/`.pdf` files; plus the operative plan. Each becomes a `document` source through the existing `RecordIngestor` chunker — stable chunk IDs, provenance-ready from the first second.
3. On save, the **GapAnalyst runs automatically** (it needs no audio) and its prioritized questions appear with their *why* (missing / stale / conflicting) and the triggering chunk, clickable exactly like any provenance chip.
4. The provider edits the question list — dismiss, reword, add — and approves it. The approved list is what question→answer alignment runs against, and dismissals are kept (a dismissed question that later proves relevant is itself a finding).
5. Interview: **Record** (big button, elapsed-time display, stop) or **Upload audio**. On completion the stage becomes `ready to generate`; the provider taps **Generate pre-op note**, watches stage progress via SSE, reviews the claim ledger (the existing ui.md workspace, unchanged), and **signs off**.

### 4.2 Intra-op
1. A (typically different) provider opens the case and lands on an **orientation view**: the signed-off pre-anaesthesia note as its claim ledger, with unanswered questions and flagged conflicts pinned to the top — the "what you need to know before induction" screen.
2. During the case they record **voice memos** — push-to-record, as many as needed, each timestamped at capture. Memos accumulate as segments of the intra-op audio source. (Streaming ASR replaces this in stretch; the memo model keeps MVP on the existing offline ASR path.)
3. **Generate intra-op record** runs event extraction (Nano → Super verification, unchanged), producing the chronological record, the events table, and anticipated issues. Review and sign-off as in pre-op.

### 4.3 Post-op
1. The recovery-area provider opens the case; **Generate handoff** composes the PACU handoff from existing claims (HandoffComposer's no-new-claims constraint unchanged — this is where it earns its keep clinically).
2. Post-op interview is recorded/uploaded like the pre-op one; the post-anaesthesia evaluation note generates from it; review and sign-off close the case.

### 4.4 Handoff acknowledge
The handoff is a transfer between two named providers, so it gets a dedicated interaction: the receiving provider opens the SBAR-shaped handoff, each claim playable/traceable as in ui.md §5.3, and taps **Acknowledge handoff**. The case records `{handoff_acknowledged_by, at}`. This is deliberately *not* a signature or a legal artifact — it demonstrates the shape of a safe transfer (received, traceable, acknowledged) on synthetic data.

### 4.5 Sign-off semantics
Sign-off asserts "I have reviewed this stage's output" and stamps `{signed_off_by, at}` on the stage. The sign-off screen surfaces what a reviewer must not miss: counts and a jump-list of `unsupported` and `conflicting` claims (which the UI already refuses to filter out by default — ui.md §5.2 invariant) and any `UNRESOLVED` provenance. Sign-off with outstanding conflicts is allowed but recorded as such. Signed-off stages become read-only; a stage can be **reopened** (recorded, prior artifacts kept — the ledger is append-only in spirit) rather than silently regenerated.

## 5. Data model & API deltas

### 5.1 Schema additions (`periop.schemas`)

Additive only — every existing case JSON must load unchanged (all new fields optional with defaults):

```jsonc
{
  // ... existing Case fields ...
  "workflow": {
    "created_by": {"provider_id": "p-lim", "name": "Dr A. Lim", "role": "consultant"},
    "created_at": "2026-07-06T09:12:00+08:00",
    "stages": {
      "preop":   {"status": "signed_off", "performed_by": "p-lim",
                  "signed_off_by": "p-lim", "signed_off_at": "…",
                  "questions_approved_at": "…"},
      "intraop": {"status": "awaiting_review", "performed_by": "p-tan"},
      "postop":  {"status": "awaiting_inputs",
                  "handoff_acknowledged_by": null, "handoff_acknowledged_at": null}
    }
  },
  "open_questions": [
    // existing shape + review fields:
    {"…": "…", "review": "approved | dismissed | edited", "edited_text": null}
  ]
}
```

- `Provider` is a tiny model `{provider_id, name, role}`; the roster is a JSON file (`data/providers.json`) editable by hand, surfaced by `GET /api/providers`. No auth: the picker is a demo affordance whose honest job is stamping attribution.
- `Source` gains optional `captured_at` and `provided_by`.
- A case without `workflow` (all existing synthetic cases) is treated as fully signed-off demo data: reviewable everywhere, writable nowhere.

### 5.2 API additions (`periop.api`)

| Endpoint | Behavior |
|---|---|
| `GET /api/providers` | Roster for the picker |
| `POST /api/cases` | Create case: `{label, provider_id}` → skeleton `Case` persisted via `CaseStore` |
| `POST /api/cases/{id}/sources/document` | Paste text or multipart file upload + document type → ingest/chunk → updated source registry. Triggers GapAnalyst when the op plan + ≥1 record exist |
| `POST /api/cases/{id}/sources/audio` | Multipart audio + kind (`preop-interview` / `intraop-notes` / `postop-interview`) → normalize to wav under `data/cases/{id}/audio/`, register/extend the audio source. Intra-op kind appends a memo; others replace-with-confirmation |
| `PUT /api/cases/{id}/questions` | Persist the reviewed question list (approve/dismiss/edit); marks the question gate passed |
| `POST /api/cases/{id}/stages/{stage}/run` | Validates gate + inputs, acquires the single-run lock, streams SSE per ui.md §7 (that section's event vocabulary is adopted verbatim), invokes the matching `pipeline.run_*` |
| `POST /api/cases/{id}/stages/{stage}/signoff` | `{provider_id}` → stamps sign-off; `409` if artifacts missing or stage already signed off |
| `POST /api/cases/{id}/stages/{stage}/reopen` | Records reopen; returns stage to `awaiting review` |
| `POST /api/cases/{id}/handoff/ack` | `{provider_id}` → stamps acknowledgment |

Conventions carried over from ui.md §4: structured JSON errors, `PERIOP_*` env vocabulary only, path-traversal rejection on anything filename-shaped, local/demo-grade (no auth, no rate limiting). New: uploads capped at a sane size (~50 MB audio, ~5 MB documents), and every write endpoint is a no-op with `409` on cases lacking a `workflow` block (seeded demo cases stay immutable).

`CaseStore` grows `save()` with atomic write (temp file + rename) — the SSE runner and the UI read the same file the writer replaces.

## 6. UX: designed for people who hate learning tech

These are requirements, testable in review, not aspirations:

1. **One primary action per case state.** Every case screen has exactly one visually dominant button, and it is always the next step in §4's lifecycle. Everything else is quiet. A provider who only ever presses the big button completes the entire workflow correctly.
2. **The screen explains itself.** Each stage panel opens with one plain sentence of status ("Interview recorded. Generate the pre-op note when ready."). No empty states without instructions; no instructions longer than a sentence.
3. **Speech first, typing last.** The only obligatory typing in the whole workflow is the case label. Records can be pasted or uploaded; everything else is spoken. Question edits are optional.
4. **Clinical vocabulary, not product vocabulary.** "Pre-op evaluation", "PACU handoff", "sign off" — never "pipeline", "artifact", "claim extraction" in provider-facing copy. (The review workspace may keep "claims" — its users are reviewing provenance and the term is load-bearing there.)
5. **Legible at arm's length.** Large tap targets (min 44 px), text labels on every action (icons never alone), the record button unmissable. The intra-op panel especially: it will be glanced at, not studied.
6. **Nothing to configure.** No settings screen, no preferences, no templates. The provider picker is the only "setup" and it is one tap.
7. **Errors say what to do.** Every failure state names the next action ("Recording failed to upload — it is kept on this device; tap Retry"), mirroring the existing 404-with-hint pattern for missing wavs.
8. **The worklist answers "what needs me".** Each row: case label, headline stage + status in words ("Intra-op — awaiting review"), who performed the last stage, and a conflict indicator. Filters: stage, status, (stretch) "my cases". The existing status-strip and audio indicators from ui.md §5.2 remain.

The three-column review workspace of ui.md is reached from any generated artifact and is unchanged — this spec adds a **stage rail** (Pre-op / Intra-op / Post-op stepper with status) above the center pane and swaps the left sidebar's case list for the worklist. Capture screens (intake form, question review, recorder) render in the center pane in place of the ledger until artifacts exist.

## 7. What this changes for evaluation — and what it must not

- The eval harness, gold artifacts, metrics, and A/B experiments of v1 §6 are untouched; they run on synthetic bundles exactly as today.
- New, cheap check worth adding: **lifecycle conformance tests** — a scripted API client walks a synthetic case through the full provider workflow (create → upload the case's own scripted records/audio → run stages → sign off) and asserts the resulting ledger matches the batch pipeline's output for the same bundle. This proves the workflow layer is a re-plumbing, not a fork, of the pipeline.
- SSE run events remain UI-facing only; NAT/OTel tracing is still the observability source of truth (ui.md §7 rule carried forward).

## 8. Testing

Same discipline as the rest of the repo (tests first, no network in CI):

- **API (pytest)**: case creation round-trip; document ingest produces chunked sources; audio upload normalization (fixture webm → wav, ffmpeg presence checked/skipped gracefully); stage gates (`409` on out-of-order run, sign-off without artifacts, writes to demo cases); question review persistence; handoff ack; atomic `CaseStore.save`; upload size caps and traversal rejection.
- **UI (vitest)**: worklist status derivation from the `workflow` block; one-primary-action rule (each lifecycle state renders exactly one primary button — this is directly assertable); recorder state machine with a mocked MediaRecorder; question review interactions; sign-off screen surfacing conflict counts; demo cases render read-only.
- **E2E (Playwright, hermetic)**: the full lifecycle walk of §7 against a fixture store with a stubbed pipeline runner (instant artifacts), including the SSE progress rendering.
- Live checks (real NIMs, real recording) stay in `scripts/`, never CI.

## 10. Risks & mitigations

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Scope creep toward a real clinical product (auth, audit, EHR) | §2 out-of-scope list is explicit; the provider picker's honest framing (attribution, not identity) is stated in-UI |
| Browser audio quality/codec variance breaks ASR | Server-side normalization to one known-good format; upload path as the always-works fallback; recording kept client-side until upload confirms |
| Long stage runs (~minutes on Super 49B) feel broken to a provider | SSE progress is MVP (§2); stage panels show per-agent progress and expected-duration copy; generation is explicitly async — provider can leave and return via the worklist |
| Writable store corrupts cases | Atomic writes; append-only spirit (reopen keeps history); demo cases immutable; store round-trip tests |
| Workflow layer drifts from the batch pipeline | Lifecycle conformance test (§7) pins them together |
| "Zero training" claims rot as features accrete | §6 is written as testable requirements; the one-primary-action rule is unit-tested; UX review against §6 is a W4 exit criterion |
| Diarization role-mapping fails on live audio | Same degradation ladder as v1 §10: heuristic → speaker-agnostic provenance; never blocks note generation |

## 11. Demo walkthrough (the three-provider story)

1. **Dr A (pre-op clinic)** creates the case, pastes the GP summary and op plan; the GapAnalyst immediately flags "records still list aspirin — confirm with patient", citing the chunk. Dr A approves the questions, records the interview, generates and signs off the pre-anaesthesia note.
2. **Dr B (theatre)** — who has never met the patient — opens the case and reads the orientation view: the conflict about aspirin is pinned at the top, and tapping it plays the patient saying she stopped it last Tuesday. Dr B records voice memos through the case and signs off the intra-op record.
3. **Dr C (recovery)** receives the PACU handoff — composed only from existing claims — plays the clip behind the PONV-risk item, and acknowledges.
4. Close on the point: in a large department running many lists in parallel, these three people may never speak. The handoff that carries its own evidence is the product.
